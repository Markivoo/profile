About

More than 14 years across business operations, MIS, finance analytics, sales operations, business analytics and enterprise BI.

Business understanding comes before the data model.

I work between business teams and technology teams. I understand the operational question behind a report, the financial logic behind a number, the source systems behind the calculation and the technology required to make the process scalable.

That perspective shapes how I approach analytics: start with the business requirement, build a reliable data foundation, validate the result and make the final information easy to trust.
