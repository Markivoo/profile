About

More than 14 years across business operations, MIS, finance analytics, sales operations, business analytics and enterprise BI.

Business understanding comes before the data model.

I work between business teams and technology teams. I understand the operational question behind a report, the financial logic behind a number, the source systems behind the calculation and the technology required to make the process scalable.

That perspective shapes how I approach analytics: start with the business requirement, build a reliable data foundation, validate the result and make the final information easy to trust.

Finance understanding. Technology execution.

I connect the financial question to the business process, ERP transaction, data structure, transformation logic, analytical model and management report.

My current work focuses on enterprise analytics, Finance and ERP data, reporting automation, reconciliation, cloud data platforms and governance. I stay hands-on with SQL, Python, PySpark, Power BI, DAX, Azure Synapse, AWS data services and enterprise systems.

The result is more than a dashboard. It is a reporting process with clear definitions, validation, controls and a reliable analytical layer for finance, operations and leadership teams.
