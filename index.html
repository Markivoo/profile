Business Context First. Data Second. Technology Where It Creates Value.

My career has been built around a simple problem: businesses generate enormous amounts of data, but the value comes from converting that data into information that people can trust and act upon.

I have spent more than 14 years working across business operations, MIS, finance analytics, sales operations, business analytics and enterprise BI. This journey has given me a combination of capabilities that sit between business teams and technology teams. I understand the operational question behind a report, the financial logic behind a number, the data structures behind the calculation, and the technology required to make the process scalable.

Finance Understanding with Technology Execution

My background gives me an unusual combination of finance-oriented business understanding and hands-on technology capability. I understand the financial question → the business process → the ERP transaction → the data structure → the transformation logic → the analytical model → the management report.

My current work focuses heavily on enterprise analytics, Finance and ERP data, reporting automation, reconciliation, cloud data platforms and governance. I work with large-volume transactional data and build processes that bring together information from multiple systems into a consistent and reliable analytical layer. At the same time, I remain hands-on with SQL, Python, PySpark, Power BI, DAX, Azure Synapse, AWS data services and enterprise systems.

My role is therefore not limited to creating dashboards. It involves understanding the business requirement, identifying the right source systems, designing the data flow, applying business logic, validating the result, establishing controls, building the reporting layer and ensuring that the final output can be relied upon by finance, operations and leadership teams. That end-to-end ownership is the foundation of how I approach analytics.
